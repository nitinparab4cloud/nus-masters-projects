AI Act Risk Navigator

Describe an AI system in plain language and get its EU AI Act risk tier, the documentation checklist that tier requires, and a cross-reference against NIST AI RMF and Singapore's governance framework.

This is a portfolio decision-support tool, not legal advice. It applies a curated, keyword-based reading of Regulation (EU) 2024/1689 and is not a substitute for a qualified legal or compliance review. Always verify against the current consolidated text of the Act.

Enter a system description on the left, or pick a sample scenario, then click Classify.
